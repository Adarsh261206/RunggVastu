05

Eight directions.
One geometry.

The directions are not superstition — they are the structure of a day. A home is only the choreography of these eight.

Touch a direction to read its character.

North — the arrival of first light. Stillness, memory, beginnings. The direction from which the day is received.

North-East — the corner where light and water agree. Clarity. The most sensitive point of any plan.

East — the day begins here. Movement, activity, the first decision of the morning.

South-East — warmth, fire, preparation. The direction of the kitchen and of appetite in every sense.

South — noon, fullness, weight. The direction of sustained effort and of rest after it.

South-West — the weight of evening. Earth, stability, storage. The direction of the conclusion.

West — the day hands itself over. Result, completion, the closing of matters.

North-West — the last light, the wind at night. Air, movement, travel, the direction of departure.
