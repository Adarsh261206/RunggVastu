05

Eight directions.
One geometry.

North is at the top — and everything turns from there, clockwise. The directions are not superstition; they are the structure of a day.

Point at a segment of the wheel — or tap a chip below — to read that direction.

उत्तरNorth
Element · WaterDeity · Kubera0°

The arrival of first light. Stillness, memory, beginnings — the direction from which the day is received, and wealth is invited in.

ईशानNorth-East
Element · WaterDeity · Ishan45°

The corner where light and water agree. Clarity, wisdom, meditation — the most sensitive point of any plan. Keep it light, keep it open.

पूर्वEast
Element · SunDeity · Indra90°

The day begins here. Movement, activity, the first decision of the morning — the direction of new opportunity and rising energy.

आग्नेयSouth-East
Element · FireDeity · Agni135°

Warmth, fire, preparation. The direction of the kitchen, of transformation — and of appetite in every sense of the word.

दक्षिणSouth
Element · EarthDeity · Yama180°

Noon, fullness, weight. The direction of sustained effort, of reputation and of rest after work is done.

नैऋत्यSouth-West
Element · EarthDeity · Nairritti225°

The weight of evening. Earth, stability, storage — the direction of relationships, sleep and the conclusion of matters.

पश्चिमWest
Element · SpaceDeity · Varuna270°

The day hands itself over. Result, completion, gain — the closing of matters and the harvest of what was begun in the east.

वायव्यNorth-West
Element · AirDeity · Vayu315°

The last light, the wind at night. Air, movement, travel and the direction of departure — of guests, journeys and letting go.
